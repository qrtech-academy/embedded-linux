\chapter{Solutions to the Exercises}
\label{sol:ch:solutions}

{\large\itshape Worked answers to everything that is argued or calculated on paper.\par}

This appendix answers every exercise that is not code: each Recall, Hand calculation and Design
exercise, and the parts of each Cross-check that are worked out by hand or argued. It is grouped by
chapter, and each answer is headed by the exercise it answers, with the same number and kind.

\textbf{The Code exercises are not answered}, here or in the repository. Each of them is checked by
running it: its Check yourself line names what passes when the code is right, and \code{make test}
runs it on the target. An answer to read would replace the work, and the work is the point. The
same goes for the half of each Cross-check that is done by machine. Where a Cross-check asks what
the machine will print, the answer here is what a driver that meets its specification prints, or
what the course measured on its own target; reconciling that with what your machine prints is
still yours to do.

\textbf{Try each exercise before you read its solution.} A Recall answer is one way of saying it,
not the only one, and an answer of yours that reaches the same facts by a different route is just
as right. Where a Design exercise asks you to choose, the solution makes a choice and gives its
reason; a different choice with a better reason is a better answer.

Every number here is either derived from the chapter that sets the exercise or was measured on the
course's target and published in that chapter, and where an answer depends on a measurement it
names the section that publishes it.
