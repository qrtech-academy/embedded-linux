\section{Review}
\label{c3:sec:review}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{What you should be able to do now}
After this chapter, you should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Say what ``monolithic kernel'' claims, and what it does not claim.
  \item Explain why the syscall interface is stable and the internal API is not, and what follows
    for a driver you maintain out of tree.
  \item Navigate to a named subsystem in the source tree without searching.
  \item Read a Kconfig entry and say under what circumstances the option will and will not appear.
  \item Say what \code{select} does that \code{depends on} does not, and why that can produce a
    broken \code{.config}.
  \item Choose \code{=y} or \code{=m} for a given driver and defend the choice.
  \item Cross-build a kernel with a config fragment, and say where each of \code{Image},
    \code{vmlinux} and the modules ends up.
  \item Measure what a config option costs, in image bytes and in modules.
\end{itemize}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{Questions to test yourself}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Why can the driver for the device holding your root filesystem not be a module?
  \item Your \code{.config} contains an option you never selected and whose \code{depends on} line
    is not satisfied. How did it get there?
  \item What is the difference between \code{vmlinux} and \code{Image}, and which one does the
    bootloader load?
  \item \code{make defconfig} for arm64 produces a configuration for how many machines? What does
    that tell you about how much of your build you are going to throw away?
  \item You backport a fix from mainline into a vendor tree that is three years old and it does not
    apply. What are your options, in order of increasing cost?
  \item Why does the kernel build its own headers rather than using the ones in \file{/usr/include}?
  \item You remove a config option contributing 34 KB and the \code{Image} shrinks by 64 KB. Where
    did the other 30 KB come from, and would removing a second 34 KB option shrink it by another 64
    KB?
\end{enumerate}

\needspace{6\baselineskip}
\subsection*{Where to look}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \file{kernel/trim.config} is where this course's build lost most of its time and size, with
    a comment on every line saying what it removed and why. It is worth reading before the lab.
\end{itemize}
